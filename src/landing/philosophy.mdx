<Principles>
<Principle id="the-onion-is-the-framework" title="The onion is the framework">Everything in Rhythm is a middleware: a function that does some work, hands control down the chain, and optionally does more work on the way back up.</Principle>
<Principle id="types-flow-with-the-request" title="Types flow with the request">When a middleware contributes something to the request context, such as a validated body, a session, or a request id, that contribution is visible in the types of every handler that runs after it.</Principle>
<Principle id="explicit-beats-magical" title="Explicit beats magical">There are no decorators, no dependency-injection containers, no file-system conventions, and no hidden execution order.</Principle>
</Principles>
